Core Features
🎣
Custom Fish System
Over 100+ unique fish across 5 rarity tiers. Each fish has configurable stats, lore, biome restrictions, time-of-day, and weather conditions.
📖
Fish Codex
A collection-based discovery system tracking species caught, completion percentage, and per-fish statistics. Cosmetic progression for dedicated anglers.
🌳
Skill Tree
6 unlockable passive skills upgraded with essence and skill points — XP multipliers, rarity bonuses, market buffs. Power comes from effort, not luck.
🌐
Web Admin Editor
Full-featured web interface with live Minecraft-style tooltip preview and lore scheme management. Create and edit fish without touching YAML or restarting.
Rarity System
Fully Configurable
Five-Tier Rarity System
Configure catch rates, loot pools, biome conditions, announcements, and sound effects per tier. Legendary catches fire server-wide broadcasts. Rarity weights stack with Bait bonuses and the Rare Finder skill node.

Progression
🪄
Totems
10 fishing buffs unlocked by level. Persistent passive bonuses that reward long-term play.
🪝
Bait System
15 consumable baits across 4 tiers. Temporarily shift loot weights for better rare catch odds.
💎
Essence Economy
Gut fish into essence currency for skills and upgrades — a self-contained loop with plugin compatibility.
Social & Events
🏆
Tournaments
Competitive events with live leaderboards. Schedule via CRON or trigger on-demand with prize-command rewards.
⚡
Dynamic Events
Fishing Frenzy, Rare Tide, Essence Surge — server-wide bonuses that drive activity spikes and player engagement.
📦
Deliveries
Fishing quests with essence rewards. Gives players daily goals and drives demand for specific fish.

Server Owner Tools
⚙️
Lore Schemes
Template system for automatic fish lore generation. Define exactly how lore looks server-wide. YAML with hot-reload — no restarts needed.
🌊

Fish spawn filtered by biome, time of day, weather, and Y-level. Gives players a real reason to explore — rare fish live in specific places.
