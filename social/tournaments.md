# Tournaments

Tournaments are competitive fishing events where players compete against each other for top spots on the leaderboard. All players participate simply by fishing during the tournament window.

## How Tournaments Work

When a tournament is active, SwagFishing tracks a specific metric for all players. At the end of the tournament, rewards are distributed to top performers.

Participation is automatic — just fish during the tournament. No sign-up is required.

## Tournament Types

| Type | What Is Tracked |
|------|----------------|
| Most fish caught | Total number of fish caught during the tournament |
| Biggest fish | Largest single fish (by size) caught |
| Rarest fish | Highest rarity fish caught |
| Total value | Combined sell value of all fish caught |

## Leaderboard

During an active tournament, you can view current standings:

```
/fish top
```

The leaderboard updates in real-time as players catch fish.

## Rewards

Rewards are distributed at the end of the tournament based on final standings. Reward types and amounts are configured by the server admin.

## Tips

* Focus your fishing strategy based on the tournament type — for a "biggest fish" tournament, target specific large fish species
* Use [Bait](../progression/bait.md), [Totems](../progression/totems.md), and [Skills](../progression/skill-tree.md) to maximize your performance
* Active [server events](../core-features/events.md) like RARE_TIDE pair well with tournaments focused on rare fish
* Check the Fish Codex to find out where rare and large fish spawn
