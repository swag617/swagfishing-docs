# Party Fishing

Party Fishing is planned for a future update.

## Status

This feature has not yet been implemented. When it arrives, this page will be updated with full documentation.

## What's Planned

Party Fishing will allow groups of players to fish together and share bonuses. Exact mechanics, XP sharing rules, and party size limits are still being designed and will be confirmed closer to release.
