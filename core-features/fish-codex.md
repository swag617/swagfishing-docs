# Fish Codex

The Fish Codex is a Pokédex-style collection tracker that lets you see every fish in the game and track which ones you've discovered.

## Opening the Codex

```
/fish codex
```

Permission: `swagfishing.codex` (default: all players)

## What Is the Codex?

The Fish Codex displays every fish that exists in SwagFishing — both base fish and custom fish — organized in a paginated 54-slot GUI. For each fish you can see:

* Fish name (colored by rarity)
* Rarity tier
* Whether you have discovered it (caught it at least once)
* Your overall collection completion percentage

Discovery happens **automatically** the first time you catch a fish. You don't need to do anything special.

## GUI Layout

The Codex uses a 54-slot paginated inventory:

* **Slots 0-44:** Fish entries
* **Slot 45:** Previous page arrow
* **Slot 49:** Info item (shows completion %)
* **Slot 53:** Next page arrow

Fish you've discovered appear with their full colored name and rarity. Fish you haven't caught yet appear grayed out, showing that they exist but haven't been found.

## Is It Only Cosmetic?

Yes. The Fish Codex is **purely cosmetic**. Completing your collection does not grant gameplay bonuses. It's designed for players who enjoy collection tracking and showing off their achievements.

Power progression in SwagFishing comes from the [Skill Tree](../progression/skill-tree.md), [Totems](../progression/totems.md), and [Bait](../progression/bait.md) — not the Codex. This means casual collectors and competitive players both enjoy the system equally.

## Tips

* Check the Codex to see what fish exist in specific biomes and conditions — useful for planning where to fish
* Ruby and Amethyst fish broadcast server-wide when caught, so you'll know when other players discover rare fish
* Your completion percentage is tracked even if you're not actively trying to complete the Codex
