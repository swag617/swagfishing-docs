# Deliveries

Deliveries are fishing quests that reward you with essence for bringing specific fish. They provide a structured way to earn essence beyond normal fishing.

## What Are Deliveries?

Deliveries are "bring me X fish" style missions. A delivery specifies one or more fish types and quantities. Complete the delivery to receive an essence reward that scales with the rarity and difficulty of the required fish.

Deliveries are accessible through the main menu (`/fish`) and integrate with the broader SwagFishing economy.

## Rewards

Delivery rewards are paid in **essence**. The amount scales based on:

* The rarity of the required fish
* The quantity required
* Your **Delivery Expert** skill level

## Delivery Expert Skill

The **Delivery Expert** skill (from the [Skill Tree](../progression/skill-tree.md)) increases essence rewards from all deliveries:

| Level | Bonus |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | +25% delivery essence |
| 2 | +50% delivery essence |

This is the only skill that specifically targets delivery rewards, making it a strong choice for players who regularly complete deliveries.

## Getting Started

1. Open the main menu with `/fish`
2. Navigate to the Deliveries section
3. View available deliveries and their requirements
4. Catch or retrieve the required fish from your Fish Bag
5. Complete the delivery to receive your essence reward

## Tips

* Keep a stock of various rarities in your Fish Bag to complete deliveries quickly
* Prioritize the Delivery Expert skill early if you plan to use deliveries as your main essence source
* Higher-rarity deliveries pay significantly more essence
